## Manifest contract law

- Manifests are executable product/canon contracts, not casual content files.
- Read the applicable schema, consumer, guard, page-family contract, Stage 1 canon, and existing identifier conventions before editing.
- Preserve canonical IDs, keys, versions, route/page ownership, ordering where contractual, asset references, surface semantics, and consumer compatibility.
- Do not invent fields, rename identifiers, silently change defaults, or repurpose an existing ID.
- Sacred Hero manifests require the exact Hero authority named by the root and Hero-scoped instructions.
- Content/design/SEO authority does not imply schema, Hero, clinical, booking, chatbot-engine, Router, or cross-repository authority.
- No PHI, patient identifiers, secrets, environment values, credentials, or unapproved clinical claims in manifests.
- New or changed manifest contracts require deterministic schema validation, relevant consumer tests, guard coverage, and migration/compatibility analysis.
- Do not weaken validation or add broad exemptions to admit invalid data.
- No merge, deploy, publication, production, or credential authority.
