- meaningful static paint without client JavaScript;
- no motion/video asset on the critical first-paint path;
- critical Hero visual transfer: mobile at most 700 KB, desktop at most 1.0 MB;
- H3.2 runtime JavaScript delta: at most 50 KB gzip above inherited V2;
- decoded visual memory: mobile at most 64 MB, desktop at most 128 MB;
- heavy raster DPR ceiling: 2.0 unless explicitly justified;
- no continuous animation or `requestAnimationFrame` requirement after settle;
- Hero-attributable CLS at most 0.02;
- preview LCP target at most 2.5 seconds under the agreed mobile profile.

Inherited V2 overruns and candidate exceptions must be measured and reported;
budgets may not be weakened silently.
