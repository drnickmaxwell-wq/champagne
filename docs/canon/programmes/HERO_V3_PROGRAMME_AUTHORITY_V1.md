## Purpose

This packet is the canonical, product-local authority for developing Hero V3 as
an isolated, phase-gated evolution of the Champagne Hero system. It grants the
current branch authority for H3.0 and, only after H3.0 is committed and
internally verified, H3.1. It does not grant H3.2 implementation, merge,
deployment, production binding, public cutover, Router, Agent, chatbot-engine or
WEOS mutation authority.

to silently redefine a higher one:

1. Founder-approved authority.

Hero V2 is the sacred ancestor and live fallback. Preserve throughout:

## Architecture-level performance and accessibility obligations

These are present-tense requirements, not H3.7 afterthoughts:

- meaningful first paint without waiting for full enhancement;
- deterministic static fallback;
- a complete reduced-motion equivalent;
- separately art-directed mobile composition;
- bounded motion intensity and attention cost;
- no obvious looping or reset seam;
- no route-navigation Hero reset;
- bounded GPU, memory and network cost;
- progressive enhancement tiers;
- deterministic approved presets;
- failure visibility and preserved accessibility semantics.

Exact numeric budgets may be refined by measured implementation evidence.

## H3.1 diagnostic obligations

The isolated lab must expose the real V2 renderer and provide:

- every static layer independently and as cumulative builds;
- complete static V2;
- every motion layer independently and full V2 motion;
- reduced-motion state;
- desktop, tablet and mobile views;
- relevant opacity, blend and mask diagnostics;
- visibility, opacity, blend, crop/position, focal and applicable phase/timing
  controls;
- current layer timing, duration and phase data;
- loop-boundary timestamps and recordings or equivalent time-indexed evidence.

The current visible seam must be diagnosed rather than assumed. Candidate causes
include intrinsic media boundaries, clustered media seams, CSS animation phase
boundaries, opacity/reveal choreography, cross-layer synchronisation, encoding,
stack remount or another measured cause. H3.1 must not repair the defect.
